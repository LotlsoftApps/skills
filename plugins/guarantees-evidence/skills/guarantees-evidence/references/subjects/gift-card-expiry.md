# gift card expiry

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

For gift cards supplied on or after 1 November 2019, Australian Consumer Law sets a minimum redemption period of three years from supply; if a card bears a shorter expiry, it is taken to be three years and a term reducing it earlier is void. For gift cards purchased after that date, post-purchase fees or charges are prohibited, and businesses do not need to honour a card after a valid expiry date has passed; the ACCC does not resolve individual disputes about gift cards or vouchers. Gift cards sold before 1 November 2019 are not affected by these changes. Consumer Affairs Victoria states that gift cards and vouchers sold for a good or service at a genuine discount are not covered by the three-year period, and that a card without an expiry date may be used for a reasonable length of time after purchase, but the claims do not establish whether that genuine-discount position overrides the statutory three-year period.

### Documented position (9)

- **[documented-position]** Australian Consumer Law section 99B(1) prohibits supplying a gift card to a consumer in trade or commerce if the day the gift card ceases to be redeemable is earlier than three years after the day of supply.
  - as of: 2026-07-01
  - quote: "99B Gift cards to be redeemable for at least 3 years"
  - quote: "A person must not, in trade or commerce, supply a gift card to a consumer if the day that the gift card ceases to be redeemable is earlier than 3 years after the day of that supply."
  - source: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00109/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_4/document_4.html
- **[documented-position]** For a gift card sold with a shorter expiry, the three-year statutory period overrides the printed date.
  - as of: 2026-07-01
  - quote: "99B Gift cards to be redeemable for at least 3 years"
  - quote: "the day that the gift card ceases to be redeemable is taken to be 3 years after the day of that supply."

- **[documented-position]** Australian Consumer Law section 99F(1) makes void any gift card term that reduces the redemption period to end earlier than three years after supply.
