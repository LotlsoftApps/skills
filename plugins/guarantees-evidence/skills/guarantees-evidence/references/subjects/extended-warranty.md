# extended warranty

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply automatically and exist despite anything a supplier or manufacturer says or does; after a warranty against defects expires, the guarantee of acceptable quality usually still applies, and a consumer can insist a supplier meet a consumer guarantee even where additional warranties cover the goods. Extended warranties are optional and usually sold separately for an extra cost; they may or may not give any extra rights beyond the automatic consumer guarantees, and regulators state that offering one that merely mirrors or is less than the guarantees can mislead consumers. CHOICE reports that many extended warranties offer no more than the free ACL rights and that 72% of surveyed claims were made when the product was under two years old; a court has imposed a $200,000 civil penalty on each of Fisher & Paykel Customer Services and Domestic & General for misleading extended warranty offers. What the claims do not establish is whether a particular extended warranty offers benefits beyond those automatic guarantees; from 9 June 2019 a separate mandatory warranty text applies to services supplied after that date.
