# sale items

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Consumers have the same legal refund rights on sale items as on full-price products, and this also applies to seconds, samples, second-hand items bought from a store or seller, and factory outlet goods. Signs or policies such as “no refund on sale items” or “exchange or credit note only for return of sale items” are unlawful and cannot remove those rights, but a consumer cannot claim a refund for a fault the seller told them about before the sale when the item was reduced because it was faulty. A change of mind is separate: businesses do not have to offer refunds or replacements for change of mind and may exclude sale items from a change of mind policy, but if a store has a change of mind refund, exchange or credit note policy it must abide by it.
