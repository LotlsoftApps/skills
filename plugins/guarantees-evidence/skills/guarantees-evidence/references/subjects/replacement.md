# replacement

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

From 9 June 2019, the mandatory text for goods and services supplied after that date says consumers can choose a refund or replacement for major failures with goods, and the ACCC states that when a product has a major problem the consumer chooses between a refund and a replacement. For a minor problem, a business does not have to offer a replacement or refund, but it can choose to do this; BIG W states that for a non-major problem it may choose a free repair instead if possible, and Kmart states that marketplace sellers may reserve the right to offer repair only for a minor failure. If a business cannot or will not repair or fix the problem within a reasonable amount of time, or at all, the consumer is entitled to a refund or replacement instead, and if an identical replacement is not reasonably available the consumer may choose a repair or a refund; replacement goods carry the same consumer guarantees as the originals. Kmart states that in some instances it may offer a repair or replacement rather than a refund, which conflicts with the ACCC position that for a major problem the consumer chooses between a refund and a replacement, and the claims do not settle whether Kmart’s statement that goods may be replaced by refurbished goods satisfies the ACCC requirement that a replacement be of the same type.
