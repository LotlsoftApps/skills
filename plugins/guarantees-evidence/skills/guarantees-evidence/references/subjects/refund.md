# refund

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

For a major problem with a product, the claims conflict on whether repair is available: the ACCC states the business must give the consumer the choice of a refund or a replacement, while another ACCC statement and BIG W's returns page also list repair. It is unlawful for a business to rely on store policies or signs that say "no refunds" or "no refunds or exchanges on sale items"; sale items carry the same legal refund rights as full-price products, and a return for a refund, repair or replacement does not require original packaging. A consumer who got what they asked for but simply changed their mind has no repair, replacement or refund right under the consumer guarantees, and a sign saying no refunds for change of mind is acceptable. A refund should be the full amount paid, must not be reduced for the consumer's use, and should be in the same form as the original payment unless the business and consumer agree otherwise; Origin Energy agreed to hand money back to over 4500 affected customers after an ACCC investigation.
