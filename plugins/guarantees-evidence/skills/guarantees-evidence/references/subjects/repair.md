# repair

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

For a minor problem, the business must repair the product for free and does not have to offer a replacement or refund, although it can choose to; for a major fault, the retailer must offer replacement, repair or refund and the consumer chooses the remedy. A business cannot tell a consumer to take the repair to the manufacturer or importer; it is generally the seller's responsibility to return products to the manufacturer for repair, and a consumer may be entitled to a remedy regardless of whether the product is under warranty. As of 1 July 2026, the Australian Consumer Law provides that a supplier required to remedy a failure may comply by repairing the goods or replacing them with goods of an identical type; as of 27 July 2026, businesses must give a written repair notice before repairing a product that can store user data, showing a sign or directing to a website is not enough, and the notice rules apply to products under $100,000 and to products over $100,000 normally bought for personal or household use. In practice, CHOICE found in 2023 that salespeople at 24 of 29 JB Hi-Fi stores and 18 of 26 Harvey Norman stores it called misrepresented consumer repair rights.
