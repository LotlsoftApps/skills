# digital products

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

From 1 November 2019, the amendments made by Schedule 1 to the Treasury Laws Amendment (Gift Cards) Act 2018 apply to gift cards supplied on or after that date. The Australian Consumer Law's definition of goods expressly includes computer software as of 2026-07-01, but Steam's stated position as of 2024-05-31 is that its content and services are licensed, not sold, and confer no title or ownership; the claims do not settle how that conflict affects a consumer's rights or which of repair, replacement or refund applies to a particular digital product failure. A documented ACCC position as of 2024-05-31 is that contracts allowing a business to unilaterally change consumer arrangements may raise unfair contract term issues, and as of that date Telstra Box Office customers wanting to keep their collection had to pay $396 upfront or a minimum of $16.50 per month to access movies they already paid for. BIG W's returns page lists e-gift cards as not eligible for change of mind returns as of 2026-08-16, and as of 2026-07-27 the ACCC states that repairs to electronic products can result in lost stored data.
