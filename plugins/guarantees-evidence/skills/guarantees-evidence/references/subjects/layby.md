# layby

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Lay-by is a sales arrangement in which a consumer pays for goods in instalments and does not receive the goods until the full price has been paid. A consumer can cancel a lay-by at any time before receiving or collecting the goods; a supplier may terminate only if the consumer breached the agreement, the supplier is no longer engaged in trade or commerce, or the products are no longer available. On termination the supplier must refund all payments made other than any termination charge, which must not be more than the supplier's reasonable costs, and except for that termination charge the supplier is not entitled to damages or any other remedy. Lay-by agreements must be in writing, transparent, legible, clearly presented, and in plain language, and businesses must provide a written copy setting out any terms and conditions including termination fees; as of 2026-07-11, the ACCC and Consumer Affairs Victoria conflict on the instalment threshold, with the ACCC stating two or more instalments and Consumer Affairs Victoria stating at least three unless the agreement is stated to be a lay-by, in which case two instalments suffice.
