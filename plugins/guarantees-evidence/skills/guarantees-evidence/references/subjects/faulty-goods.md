# faulty goods

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; the ACCC and the state regulators rewrite their guidance without announcing it, and this corpus found three of them publishing different maximum penalties at the same time.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

For faulty goods under the Australian Consumer Law, a minor failure must be repaired or fixed for free and the business does not have to offer a replacement or refund, while a major failure entitles the consumer to a replacement or refund, even if the consumer first agreed to a repair. Return in original packaging is not required, businesses must not tell consumers to go to the manufacturer, and a business that confirms a fault must reimburse reasonable return costs; if it finds no fault it may charge collection and inspection costs only after giving a reasonable estimate. The monetary threshold is $100,000 for goods purchased from 1 July 2021 and $40,000 for goods purchased before that date. Faulty, unsafe or poor-quality products were reported by 20% of consumers in 2023, down from 30% in 2016, and the claims do not specify the situations in which the consumer gets to choose the remedy.
