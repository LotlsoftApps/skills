# notice to vacate

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Since 25 November 2025, Victorian rental providers cannot issue a notice to vacate without a valid reason, including at the end of a fixed-term agreement; from that date, a fixed-term agreement automatically becomes a periodic month-by-month lease unless the parties agree to a new fixed term or the provider gives a notice to vacate for a valid reason. Certain notices to vacate require 90 days' notice, including a notice given because the property is to be sold; a notice based on at least 14 days of rent arrears has a 14-day minimum notice period, and an immediate notice is available if the renter or their visitor intentionally or recklessly causes serious damage. A notice to vacate is not valid if it is unlawful discrimination or if required supporting evidence is not supplied, such as a witnessed statutory declaration for a moving-in notice or a building permit for a renovations notice. Rental providers cannot give notice for requesting repairs, asking to have a pet, or challenging a rent increase, and if the renter does not leave after notice, the provider may apply for a possession order.
