# rental minimum standards

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Victorian rental minimum standards apply if a renter moved in on or after 29 March 2021, but they do not apply where a rental agreement became a periodic agreement before 29 March 2021; renewing a fixed-term lease or changing to a periodic lease after that date does not restart the lease. From 29 March 2022 all Victorian rental properties must have curtains or blinds fitted to each window in any room likely to be used as a bedroom or living area; from 29 March 2023 rental agreements entered into from that date require an energy-efficient fixed heater in the main living area, although ConditionHQ states the fixed heater must be energy efficient without that agreement date limit. From 25 November 2025 Victorian rental properties must meet the minimum standards before they are advertised or offered, and it is an offence for a landlord or their agent to advertise or offer a property for rent that does not meet them; from 1 December 2025 all corded internal window coverings in Victorian rental properties must have an anchor installed to secure the cords and prevent them forming loops, and rooming house operators must install a fixed heater secured to a wall, floor or ceiling in each resident's room. The standards also cover external entry doors with functioning deadlocks or locks that can be unlocked with a key from the outside and without one from the inside, rooms free from mould and damp caused by or related to the building structure, and supply of a rubbish bin and a recycling bin; RACV asserts failure can incur penalties of more than $11,000 for an individual and over $57,000 for a company, the material conflicts on timing with Tenants Victoria dating an additional standard to 20 March 2023 while Consumer Affairs Victoria states the electrical safety standard commenced on 29 March 2023, and new energy efficiency standards begin in phases from 1 March 2027.
