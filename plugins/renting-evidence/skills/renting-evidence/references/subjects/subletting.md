# subletting

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Victorian renters must ask the rental provider for written permission to sub-let, and the provider must not unreasonably withhold consent or charge a fee; in public housing the provider can refuse permission because subletting would be unfair to others waiting. If someone sub-lets without permission, the rental provider can end the agreement or give a 14-day notice to vacate, and a provider who asks for an additional fee for consent faces a fine of 60 penalty units. A head renter who sub-lets takes on the rental provider's responsibilities, must advise a prospective sub-renter if they are not the owner, must lodge any bond with the RTBA within 10 business days, and any subletting agreement signed after 29 March 2021 is subject to minimum standards. For a sub-renter to be protected, the arrangement requires exclusive possession of some or all of the property and the landlord's written consent or a VCAT order; in 2016 a Supreme Court case held renters breached their agreement by renting on Airbnb, reversing a VCAT decision that it was a licence not a sublease, although one 2016 source said landlords could not rely on the RTA subletting provisions to evict Airbnb-listing tenants.
