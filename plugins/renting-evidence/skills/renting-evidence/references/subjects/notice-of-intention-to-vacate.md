# notice of intention to vacate

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

From 25 November 2025, Victorian rental providers cannot issue a notice to vacate without a valid reason, even at the end of a fixed-term agreement, and notice periods for certain rental provider notices to vacate and rent increases lengthened from 60 to 90 days across rental properties, rooming houses, caravan parks and residential parks. A renter's notice of intention to vacate must be in writing; the usual minimum notice is 28 days, with 14 days or immediate notice allowed in some situations, and more notice is allowed but not less, while leaving early still means rent is owed for the whole notice period. For a fixed-term agreement, the vacate date cannot be before the agreement's end date; an earlier date is breaking the agreement and may mean lease break fees. If a renter ends the lease with a valid reason and the correct notice period, no additional rent or compensation can be demanded, but sources conflict on the form of written notice: Aussie Rent Laws says a clear written notice is sufficient, while Landlord Wise says a letter, email or text message is not valid even with all required information.
