# rent bidding

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Rental bidding was prohibited in Victoria as part of the 2021 rental law reforms, and the reforms introduced the requirement that rental properties be advertised at a fixed price. Holding rental auctions and inviting rental bids became offences for properties offered for rent from 29 March 2021, and from 25 November 2025 accepting rental bids above the advertised price is prohibited, including unsolicited offers. A price range or "Contact agent" in an advertisement is a form of rental bidding; enforcement has included fines against more than 40 agencies, more than 50 infringements totalling over $600,000 in 2024-25, and criminal prosecutions against five estate agencies. As of 2026 the claims conflict on voluntary higher offers: Soho Real Estate states that tenants can still offer more, while Consumer Affairs Victoria states that agents and rental providers are banned from accepting offers to pay rent higher than the advertised amount.
