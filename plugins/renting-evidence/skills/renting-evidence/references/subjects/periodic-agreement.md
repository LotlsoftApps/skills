# periodic agreement

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

When a fixed-term Victorian rental agreement ends and the renter remains, it automatically becomes a periodic month-to-month agreement unless the renter gives notice to leave, the parties agree to a new fixed term, or the rental provider gives a notice to vacate for a valid reason. In a periodic agreement, a rental provider cannot give notice to vacate without a valid reason. From 29 March 2021 certain terms are prohibited in new periodic rental agreements, and Victoria's rental minimum standards do not apply to an agreement that became periodic before 29 March 2021. Rent increase timing is not fully settled across sources: Consumer Affairs Victoria and other sources say a periodic agreement that started before 19 June 2019 may be increased once every 6 months, while one that became periodic on or after 19 June 2019 may be increased once every 12 months, but Collings Real Estate says the 12-month limit applies to periodic agreements regardless.
