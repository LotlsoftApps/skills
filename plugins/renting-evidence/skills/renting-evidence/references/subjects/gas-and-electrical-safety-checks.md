# gas and electrical safety checks

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

For rental agreements entered into after 29 March 2021, gas safety checks are required every two years by a licensed or registered gasfitter endorsed in the specialised class of Type A Gas Appliances Servicing, and all electrical installations and fittings must be checked by a licensed electrician at least once every two years. From 13 October 2026, the two-year gas and electrical safety check requirement applies to all rental agreements, including those commencing before that date; from 29 March 2023 all power outlets and lighting circuits in rental premises in Victoria must connect to both a switchboard-type circuit breaker and a switchboard-type residual current device. Consumer Affairs Victoria states that rental providers who entered into an agreement before 29 March 2021 are not legislated to conduct gas safety checks, so before 13 October 2026 the gas duty is not legislated for those older agreements; the claims do not settle whether electrical safety checks applied to those older agreements before that date. A rental provider must disclose the date of the last gas safety check and any outstanding recommendations before entering an agreement, and must provide a copy of the most recent gas safety check within 7 days after a written request; one source says each gas and electrical check record must be provided proactively within seven days, which conflicts.
