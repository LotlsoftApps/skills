# urgent repairs

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Urgent repairs in Victoria include faults or damage that make the property unsafe or insecure, failure of an essential service or appliance, mould or damp caused by or related to the building structure, and a property that does not meet minimum standards. The deadline for a rental provider to act is reported inconsistently: immediately, within 24 hours, within 48 hours, or within 2 to 3 days. A renter can arrange and pay for urgent repairs, but only up to $2,500, and must give written notice asking for repayment within 7 days of the repairs being made while continuing to pay rent; repair disputes must go through Rental Dispute Resolution Victoria first. From 13 October 2026, rental providers and their agents must arrange gas and electrical safety checks every two years for all Victorian rental agreements.
