# condition report

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

In Victoria, all rental properties, rooming houses, caravans, caravan sites and Part 4A park sites must have condition reports. The rental provider or agent must sign the report and give two copies, or one if sent electronically, to the renter before move-in; the renter has five business days from moving in to return it, and if no report was given before move-in the renter can complete their own and should give it to the provider or agent within five business days. From 29 March 2021 the Consumer Affairs Victoria template applies to all Victorian properties rented out from that date, and for rental agreements entered into from that date a returned report acts as written notice that repairs are needed, the right to apply to VCAT to amend the report applies only to those agreements, and extra professional cleaning requirements apply to agreements signed from that date. Justice Connect advises renters to list every repair issue and return the report within five business days to avoid liability for pre-existing damage; at the end of the agreement the provider or agent must complete the exit condition report section within 10 days with the renter present or given a reasonable opportunity, and Justice Connect states that in an end-of-occupancy dispute the Tribunal must find the facts in the report proven, while separately stating that the conclusive-evidence presumption does not apply where issues could not reasonably be discovered on a reasonable inspection.
