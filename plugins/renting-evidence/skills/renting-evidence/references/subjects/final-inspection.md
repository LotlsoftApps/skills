# final inspection

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

At the end of a Victorian rental agreement the rental provider, property manager or agent does a final inspection, and the renter must be present or have been given a reasonable opportunity to be there. The property must be left reasonably clean and as it was at the start of the lease except for fair wear and tear, and rental providers cannot claim bond for fair wear and tear, but the claims do not settle one uniform cleaning standard: Tenants Victoria says a renter need not steam clean carpets if the property is already reasonably clean even if the lease says otherwise, while commercial cleaning sources say every nook and cranny must be clean and that a professional receipt may improve the chance of a full bond refund. Renters can claim their bond directly with the Residential Tenancies Bond Authority without the rental provider's agreement and with no time limit, and from 1 July 2026 they can move their bond to their next rental home under the Portable Rental Bond Scheme.
