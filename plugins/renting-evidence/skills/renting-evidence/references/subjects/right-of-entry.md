# right of entry

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

In Victoria, a rental provider or their agent may enter only for a listed reason, between 8 am and 6 pm on days that are not public holidays, and must give written notice stating the reason. For a private rental, a routine inspection requires at least 7 days written notice, may be done only after the first 3 months and not more than every 6 months; showings to prospective renters are capped at twice a week and one hour each time, a sales inspection requires payment of $30 or half a day's rent, whichever is greater, and the renter does not have to leave during an inspection or showing. Rooming house residents generally must receive at least 24 hours' written notice, with at least 48 hours for a routine inspection as of 12 August 2026, and a general room inspection is allowed only once every four weeks. There are conflicts: Aussierentlaws.com states routine inspections need at least 24 hours' written notice and commonly occur every three months, while other claims state at least 7 days written notice for a private rental routine inspection and general inspections every 6 months at most; also, a 2015 VCAT decision found no right to open house inspections, but a 2026 documented position says open for inspections cannot happen more than twice a week or last more than an hour.
