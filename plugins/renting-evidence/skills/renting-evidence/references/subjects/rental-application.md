# rental application

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

From 29 March 2021, any Victorian rental application form must include a statement about unlawful discrimination, and holding rental auctions or inviting renters to bid is an offence; from 31 March 2026, all private rental applications must be made using the prescribed Consumer Affairs Victoria form. A rental provider or agent cannot charge an application fee, a credit check fee, or a fee to show the property, cannot ask about pets, prior legal action or disputes, or bond history, cannot ask for information beyond the standard application form, and can request no more than two identity documents and no more than two capacity-to-pay documents. The provider or agent must protect the applicant’s personal information, use it only for suitability or another legitimate reason, tell the applicant which tenancy databases they use, and refund a holding deposit once both parties sign the agreement. There is a conflict on identification: Consumer Affairs Victoria caps identity documents at two, while Urban Renters tells applicants that most agents will ask for identification adding up to around 100 points.
