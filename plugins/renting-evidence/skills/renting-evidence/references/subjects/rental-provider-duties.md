# rental provider duties

Compiled 2026-08-16. Every claim carries the date its quote was true; renting law is amended and the regulator rewrites its guidance, both without announcing it.

## Summary

Written from the claims below and checked against them.

Victorian rental providers must organise and pay for repairs that are not the renter’s fault, make urgent repairs immediately, and make non-urgent repairs within 14 days of a written request; renters must continue paying rent while waiting and cannot organise and pay more than $2500 for urgent repairs themselves. Minimum standards apply to a renter who moved in on or after 29 March 2021, additional standards were introduced on 29 March 2022, 20 March 2023 and 1 December 2025, and from 25 November 2025 a property must meet the minimum standards before it is advertised, with advertising or offering a non-compliant property an offence; for rental agreements entered into from 29 March 2023 the fixed heater in the main living area must be energy efficient, and from 1 December 2025 all rental properties must have secured cords on internal window coverings. From 13 October 2026 rental providers and their agents must arrange gas and electrical safety checks by a qualified tradesperson every two years for all rental agreements, annual smoke alarm safety checks apply to all rental agreements from 29 March 2021, and new minimum rental standards for energy efficiency take effect from 1 March 2027. Where the minimum standards do not apply because a lease started before 29 March 2021 the landlord still has a legal duty to ensure the property is fit and safe to live in.
